What to do now

  1. Protect yourself. Cover your head and move away from things that can fall.
  2. When the shaking stops, turn off fire. If you are near the sea, move to higher ground.
  3. Evacuate following official instructions. Do not decide alone.

This app relays warnings and information issued by the Japan Meteorological Agency. For evacuation, follow instructions from your local municipality.

This app does not provide evacuation orders or information on whether evacuation shelters are open.

Emergency numbers

  • 🚔 Police: 110
  • 🚒 Fire service / Ambulance: 119 Emergency calls for fires, sudden illness or injuries.
  • 🌊 Coast Guard: 118
  • 📱 Japan Visitor Hotline (24h): 050-3816-2787 Help in emergencies such as illness or disasters. Available 24/7 in English, Chinese, Korean and Japanese.
  • 📞 Disaster Message Dial: 171 The 171 telephone service is available when NTT activates it. Check the official guidance for availability and conditions.
